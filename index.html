📖 Project: A REAL TASTE OF THE WILD (Tanzania)

A REAL TASTE OF THE WILD

Serengeti National Park is the most well-known wildlife conservation park in Tanzania and is a great traveling experience. In the park, you can see about thirty-five different species of wild animals, such as lions, cheetahs and giraffes. The most fascinating thing happens between late June and September. About a million gnus and two hundred thousand zebras move to the northern part of the park to find areas with more water and food.


There are plenty of things to do in the Serengeti as well. You can rent a jeep and take safari rides to watch animals in their natural habitat, or take pictures of animals crossing the Mara River.
